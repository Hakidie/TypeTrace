# Sources of `words.txt` (Nối từ)

`words.txt` is built by `minigame-words/build-noi-tu.mjs` from:

- **Viet74K and Viet11K**, Vietnamese word lists, https://github.com/duyet/vietnamese-wordlist.
  Licence: GNU GPL v2.0 (full text in `LICENSE-Viet74K.txt`).
- **Leipzig Corpora Collection**, Vietnamese corpora `vie_news_2022`, `vie_wikipedia_2021`, `vie_mixed_2014` and
  `vie-vn_web_2015`, https://wortschatz-leipzig.de. Licence: CC BY 4.0.
  D. Goldhahn, T. Eckart, U. Quasthoff: *Building Large Monolingual Dictionaries at the Leipzig Corpora Collection:
  From 100 to 200 Languages*. LREC 2012.

Only two-syllable words and counts of syllable pairs are taken from the corpora. No sentences are copied.
